FLAME-PROOF MOTORS · EX ‘DB’

Protected for
hazardous areas.

Introduction

Flame-proof motors are recommended where explosive atmospheres may arise from inflammable gases, dust, or airborne particles. Their construction prevents internal sparks from escaping the motor enclosure and contacting the surrounding atmosphere. LHP offers IE2, IE3 and IE4 flame-proof motors compliant with IS/IEC/EN 60079-1, for Zones 1, 2, 21 and 22; gas groups I, IIA, IIB and IIC; and temperature classes up to T6. Flame-proof brake motors and single-phase versions are also available.

BRAKE MOTORS

Controlled stopping
when it matters.

Introduction

Brake motors are used where reliable, safe stopping for short durations is required, such as on packaging lines. LHP brake motors use fail-safe electromagnetic brakes mounted on the rear end-shield. The DC brakes engage when the power supply is switched off, with rectifiers in the terminal box converting the AC supply to DC for brake operation.

CRANE & HOIST DUTY MOTORS

Built for
intermittent duty.

Introduction

Crane and hoist duty motors are designed for demanding intermittent-duty drives that need high starting torque with low starting current. They withstand frequent start-stop and reversal operations. Their duty rating defines the complete load cycle, including starting, electric braking, no-load and de-energized rest periods. LHP offers S1 through S9 duty options in accordance with IS 12824.

INVERTER DUTY MOTORS

VFD-ready
drive performance.

Introduction

Inverter duty motors are recommended when a Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) is used to control process parameters. Their special construction withstands VFD voltage spikes and supports very low-speed operation without overheating. LHP tests inverter-duty motors with VFDs at its KLPL- and PESO-approved Test Lab. Available in IE2, IE3 and IE4 efficiencies, they feature insulated bearings, dual-coated high-grade copper winding wires, 100% vacuum-pressure impregnation, and optional force cooling after application review.

ROLLER TABLE MOTORS

Built for
steel processing.

Introduction

Roller Table Motors are specially developed for conveyors that move steel billets through roller mills in steel plants. They comply with IPSS No. 1-03-007-14 and are available up to 450 frame size in 4, 6 and 8 poles, for ambient temperatures from −35°C to +70°C. Circular-fin frames and options such as online greasing, PTC thermistors, RTD winding sensors, BTD bearing sensors, encoders and electromagnetic brakes support demanding steel-mill operation.

VARIABLE SPEED GEAR MOTOR

Precise speed control
for compact drives.

Introduction

Lubi Variable Speed Gear Motors use analog or digital speed controllers to provide wide-range speed adjustment: 90 to 1300 r/min at 50 Hz and 90 to 1600 r/min at 60 Hz. Variable speed, braking, forward/reverse running, and soft-start/soft-stop functions are available. The built-in tacho-generator provides feedback control to maintain a constant speed despite frequency changes.
