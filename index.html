ABOUT STALWART TECHNOWARE

Engineering reliability.
Powering performance.

Stalwart Technoware is an industrial solutions company specialising in electric motors and industrial gear motors, delivering reliable power transmission solutions for a wide range of industrial applications.

Established in 2022, we bring together technical expertise, application understanding, and a strong commitment to customer service. As an Authorised Channel Partner of LHP Motors, we provide quality motor solutions backed by professional support and industry-focused expertise.

Our foundation is built on 8+ years of experience in electric motors and geared motors across sales and service, complemented by practical experience in industry-related operations.

We believe in quality, reliability, trust, and long-term partnerships—working alongside customers from product selection and root-cause identification through to effective resolution.
